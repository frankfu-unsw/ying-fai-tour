How this was built
  • Window heights come from the sales brochure: 2/F is +57.55 mPD and each floor adds 2.75 m. Eye level is set 1.5 m above the floor.
  • The terrain is public SRTM-based elevation data at about 30 m resolution near the site and 100 m further out. Sea beyond 7 km east is assumed open. Dark blue patches in the overview mark the sea that the chosen window can see.
  • Block positions and the east wing's facing come from the brochure's key plan and typical floor plan. Flats 2 and 3 sit at the tip of the east wing.
  • Acoustic fins follow the typical floor plan: flat 2 has one on the north edge of its living-room window on every floor (2/F to 39/F), flat 3 one on the south edge up to 25/F. The fin depth of about 1.5 m is measured off the plan, and the window opening (4.5 m wide, sill 0.6 m, head 2.3 m) is approximate.
  • The seen-sea figures count only what the living-room window shows from where you stand. The small east-facing bedroom bay window on the north side of flat 2 (south side of flat 3) is not modelled.
  • Village houses, the film studio and trees are not modelled unless you tick the tree option. Earth curvature is included.
  • Read results as plus or minus one or two floors. The HA price list adds extra premium for flats 2 and 3 between 22/F and 24/F, which matches where the bay opens up once trees are allowed for.
