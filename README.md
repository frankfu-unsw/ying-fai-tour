# Ying Fai Court flat views

3D pages for Ying Fai Court (影輝苑), Tseung Kwan O, HOS 2025:

- `index.html`: walk-through of any flat in Blocks A and B on any floor of the typical plan (Block A 3/F to 39/F, Block B 4/F to 38/F; flats 2 and 3 from 2/F, 9 and 10 from 3/F). Flats 2, 3, 9 and 10 have three furnished layouts of three or four bedrooms plus the bare flat; every other flat is built from the sales brochure's typical floor plan, bare or with simple furniture. All of them have doors that open as you walk through (or can be hidden), the real view from each window, the sun at any time of day (its path, the light and shadows it casts inside, and when each window gets direct sun), where the lifts and refuse room are on the floor, the common corridors of the floor (step out of the lift into the lobby, walk along the corridors to your front door, or walk up to any other flat's front door to go into that flat), and an area view that zooms out to the whole of Hong Kong, from Lantau, the airport and Tuen Mun to Tung Ping Chau and from the Shenzhen border down to Po Toi (terrain, coastline, main roads, tunnels, place names, and buildings and the names of estates, buildings and roads streamed in round wherever you look, loaded at runtime from the AWS Terrain Tiles, OpenStreetMap via OpenFreeMap, and the Lands Department's map labels). Links can pick the flat and floor, e.g. `index.html#A3-24` or `index.html#B17-12`.
- `block-a.html`: what Block A flats 2 and 3 can see from every floor, including the acoustic fins.
- `block-b.html`: what Block B flats 9 and 10 can see from every floor.

Each page is a single self-contained HTML file (three.js and fonts load from public CDNs). Every residential floor of both towers is built from the typical floor plans in the HA sales brochure (Block A 3/F to 39/F and Block B 4/F to 38/F): each wall, window, door and room of all 44 flats, read straight off the brochure's vector drawings by `src/tools/plan/pipeline.py` into the `PLAN` data in `src/common.js.html`. Block A 2/F and Block B 3/F, just below the typical floors, are drawn with the typical plan too, and the small changes on Block A 7/F, 16/F and 25/F (a water meter cabinet and pressure-reducing valve tucked into flats 5, 6, 16 and 18) are left out; room layouts, window heights, podium levels and partition positions are estimates from the HA sales brochure and booklet. Any partition needs HA approval before it is built.
