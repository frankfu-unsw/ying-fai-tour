How this was built
  • Walls, rooms and windows follow the Block A typical floor plan (3/F to 39/F) in the HA sales brochure. Sizes come from its dimension strings; window sill and head heights are estimates.
  • 36/F is at about +151.05 mPD (2/F at +57.55 and 2.75 m per floor). Eye level is 1.55 m above the floor.
  • Plans A, B and C add light 75 mm partitions. In plan B the master bedroom's new wall meets the east window part-way along its north half, so it has to land on a window frame post; where the real posts are may move it by a few hundred millimetres. Any partition needs HA approval under the flat's decoration guidelines before you build it.
  • A room with no window, like the helper's room in plan B, needs mechanical ventilation such as an extractor fan or air-con.
  • The acoustic fin sits in line with the north wall and sticks out 1.5 m past the east facade.
  • Outside, the terrain is SRTM-based elevation data at about 30 m resolution near the site and 100 m further out. Village houses, trees (unless ticked) and islands more than 7 km away are not modelled.
  • Block B is placed from the brochure's key plan and shaped from its typical floor plan. Main roads come from OpenStreetMap and are laid on the terrain, so flyovers and cuttings show at ground level; tunnels are left out. Zoom out, or pick Area, to see them with the nearby places.
  • "Degrees of sea" counts the compass directions in which you can see sea through a window from where you stand, after walls, the fin, the neighbouring flats and the hills. Window figures are taken about 1.5 m back from the glass where the room allows. Bedroom and bathroom doors count as shut: inside one of those rooms only its own window counts, and from elsewhere you can't see through it.
